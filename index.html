Nobody should have to babysit a dashboard: How alerting works in Cribl Insights | Cribl

Cribl Insights now features alerting capabilities to proactively notify teams of anomalies without manual dashboard monitoring.

  • Alerting is built around three components: Monitors (define “normal” and thresholds), Alerts (record instances when data deviates), and Notifications (deliver alerts to existing team communication channels like Slack, email, or PagerDuty).
  • Monitors can be enabled from pre-defined defaults or customized using Cribl metrics and thresholds.
  • Alerts are recorded in Insights for historical review, enabling analysis of alert duration and severity.
  • Notifications are context-rich, providing details about the triggering event directly within team workflows, reducing noise and ensuring actionable insights.

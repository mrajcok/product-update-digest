Cortex ITDR: Detecting Cyber threats in Google Workspace - Palo Alto Networks Blog

  • Palo Alto Networks Cortex XSIAM now features Identity Threat Detection and Response (ITDR) capabilities for Google Workspace, addressing the increasing shift of attacks targeting the SaaS identity layer.
  • The platform detects attacks leveraging “living off the land” techniques—such as OAuth consent phishing, credential harvesting via the Drive API (using tools like GD-Thief), and abuse of legitimate features—that bypass traditional security controls.
  • Cortex XSIAM establishes behavioral baselines for users and entities within Google Workspace, identifying deviations from normal activity (e.g., unauthorized app access, unusual file access patterns) and correlating multiple signals into high-severity incidents.
  • Specific alerts are mapped to MITRE ATT&CK techniques, covering areas like domain policy modification, credential access, data exfiltration, and impairment of defenses, offering comprehensive threat coverage within Google Workspace.
